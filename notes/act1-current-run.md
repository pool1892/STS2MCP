## Current Strategic Priorities

- Build enough front-loaded damage for hallway fights and elites.
- Preserve HP for at least one elite if the map offers a reasonable path.
- Prefer card rewards that solve immediate Act 1 problems: efficient damage, vulnerable/scaling, or high-value block that does not slow the deck too much.

## Tactical Notes

- Re-read state after every card play that changes hand indexes.
- In combat, use enemy intent first: kill if lethal is available, block only for meaningful incoming damage.
- Spend potions aggressively if they prevent a large HP loss or secure an elite/boss fight.
- Against Vantom specifically, do not spend the biggest single-hit attacks into Slippery unless no better play exists. Use multi-hit cards, cheap Strikes, thorns, and draw-triggered attacks to clear Slippery, then convert to the normal damage plan.
- Once Hellraiser and Stampede are both active, draw and "leave an attack in hand" become tactical resources. Sometimes the best play is a block/draw card that enables a free attack rather than manually spending energy on the attack.
